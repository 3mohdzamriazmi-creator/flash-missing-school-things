# Number Football — Year 1
30 English number spelling questions (one to twenty). Eight supplied worksheet targets kept first: eleven, thirteen, fifteen, seventeen, eighteen, ten, twelve, fourteen; twelve’s incorrect “five and five” clue corrected to “ten and two”. Twelve added questions cover remaining numbers. First letter given; tap shuffled remaining letters, remove or clear, check spelling then choose a penalty direction. Every solved word earns one spelling mark and unlocks one penalty shot. Aim away from the moving goalkeeper: a goal earns one additional mark; a save earns zero. Total /60: spelling /30 plus goals /30. Retries can earn the spelling mark. Malay clues, read-aloud, review/replay, touch/keyboard buttons and reduced-motion support. Local HTML and recording.js, no external artwork. Open index.html locally. Name/class form and score recording bridge included; endpoint awaits teacher setup. Top five shows best scores per pupil in the same class, with own rank and tied ranks. See teacher-setup/year-1-number-football. Nothing published. Upload index.html, recording.js and README.md together inside year-1-number-football/ without changing root files.

Includes ten additional counting questions for numbers 1–10, with football pictures.
