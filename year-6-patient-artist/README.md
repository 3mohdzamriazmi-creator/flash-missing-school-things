# The Patient Artist: Year 6 Reading Quest

Open index.html in a modern browser. A self-contained, phone-friendly reading game by Mr. Z. All styling, scripts and storybook artwork are embedded. No installation, account or external assets are required.

The passage and questions 10–14 are transcribed from the three screenshots provided by Mr. Z. Correct answers inferred from the passage: A, B, B, C, C. Question 10 uses the best available choice in the worksheet: Cheng Li took too long. The emperor waited three years, then became furious when the final painting took two minutes.

Read the four-paragraph story first. Optional Malay translations are available for the passage and each question. During a question, open Read the passage to consult the complete story. Select A, B or C. A correct first attempt earns one star. Wrong choices are disabled; a hint permits a retry without earning a star. Once solved, all answers lock. Results are out of 10 and Review Answers explains each answer. Play Again resets all progress.

Keyboard: Tab/Shift+Tab navigate; Enter/Space activate buttons and passage toggles. Sound effects begin off. Read aloud reads the English story on the story screen and the question with choices during gameplay. Browser voice availability varies. Reduced-motion preferences disable decorative animation.

For GitHub Pages, upload this entire folder to the existing Pages source root as year-6-patient-artist/. Do not replace the repository root index.html. No upload or deployment has been performed.

Verification: automated Chrome tests cover the passage, ten answers, translations, perfect/all-retry/mixed scoring, retry and duplicate protection, reset, review, keyboard passage toggle, and responsive widths 320/375/390/768/1440. Actual audio, physical touch, screen-reader output and other browser engines require manual testing.

Original three-scene illustration added using the built-in ImageGen tool, embedded in index.html for offline use and simple uploads. Saved source artwork: ../artwork/patient-artist-story.png.
