# Word Detective: Year 6 English

Open index.html in a modern browser. All CSS, JavaScript and SVG art are embedded; no backend, build step or external assets are required.

30 spelling cases in six categories (five each): Crime, Animals, School, Food, Places and Jobs. The five Crime cases are based on Mr. Z's supplied worksheet (questions 21–25): mask, clue, robbery, thief, report. The example is crime. The bottom worksheet clue was partially obscured, and has been transcribed as: “A policeman wrote down about what happened to the victims.” The answer report is inferred from the clue and six-letter R pattern.

Type the full word or just the missing letters. Capital letters and surrounding spaces are accepted. Two-word answers accept spaces or no spaces; spaces are shown as gaps rather than letter boxes. Blank submissions do not count as attempts. A correct first attempt without a hint earns one mark; retries or hints make that case practice-only. Once solved, the case locks. Final score is out of 30. Review Words shows every clue, answer and meaning; Play Again resets all progress.

Phone layouts, keyboard controls, optional sound, read-aloud fallback and reduced-motion support are included. Sound effects start off. Read Clue is a separate explicit speech request.

QA passed in headless Chrome: all 30 words and six categories; scores 30/30, 0/30 and 15/30; uppercase and missing-letter input; retries and hints; blank submissions; duplicate protection; reset; review; keyboard; widths 320, 375, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels without horizontal overflow. Actual audio, physical touch, screen readers and other browser engines remain untested.

For GitHub Pages, add this entire folder inside the existing Pages source root. The deployed path will be year-6-word-detective/. Keep existing root files unchanged. Nothing has been uploaded or published.

Malay translations appear beneath all 30 English clues, including in Review Words. Read Clue continues to read the English clue.
