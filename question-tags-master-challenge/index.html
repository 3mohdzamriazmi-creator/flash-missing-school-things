How do question tags work?

A question tag is a short question at the end of a sentence. We use it to check if something is true.

Question tag ialah soalan pendek di hujung ayat untuk memastikan sesuatu itu benar.

Rule 1 of 6

Match the helping verb and the pronoun.

Ali → he · your sister → she · the pupils → they.
“Is” goes with “isn't”; “can” goes with “can't”.

Padankan kata bantu dan kata ganti nama dengan ayat.
