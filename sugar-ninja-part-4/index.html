A

Penny 🍎

Penny watched a documentary about healthy eating and was shocked by the amount of sugar in bread and soup. She blames advertisers for making unhealthy food look so delicious. Penny believes it is very difficult to know how much sugar is added because food labels are too hard to understand.

BM: Penny menonton sebuah dokumentari tentang pemakanan sihat dan terkejut dengan jumlah gula di dalam roti dan sup. Dia menyalahkan pengiklan kerana membuatkan makanan tidak sihat kelihatan begitu sedap. Penny percaya amat sukar untuk mengetahui berapa banyak gula ditambah kerana label makanan terlalu sukar untuk difahami.
B

Bart 🍊

Bart thinks the media always tries to scare people about different kinds of food. He believes people are clever enough to make their own choices. In his opinion, a small amount of sugar can give us energy when we are tired, and it is healthy to eat a wide variety of food groups instead of cutting items out completely.

BM: Bart berpendapat pihak media sentiasa cuba menakutkan orang ramai tentang pelbagai jenis makanan. Dia percaya orang ramai cukup bijak untuk membuat pilihan sendiri. Pada pendapatnya, sedikit gula boleh memberi kita tenaga apabila penat, dan adalah sihat untuk makan pelbagai kumpulan makanan daripada meninggalkan terus sesuatu makanan.
C

Minnie 🥝

Minnie was worried when she discovered that one can of fizzy cola has seven teaspoons of sugar. Her mother is also concerned about eating too much sweet food. Minnie now uses an app on her phone to scan the barcodes on items, which helps her family check exactly how much sugar is inside before they buy anything.

BM: Minnie berasa bimbang apabila mendapati satu tin kola bergas mempunyai tujuh sudu teh gula. Ibunya juga bimbang tentang pengambilan terlalu banyak makanan manis. Minnie kini menggunakan aplikasi pada telefonnya untuk mengimbas kod bar pada barangan, yang membantu keluarganya menyemak jumlah sebenar gula di dalamnya sebelum membeli apa-apa.

🛠️ How to connect your Google Sheet (Takes 1 minute):
  1. Open a new Google Sheet, click Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Delete any code inside, paste the copied script below, and click Save (💾).
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment, choose type Web app, set "Who has access" to Anyone, click Deploy, and paste the Web app URL above!
function doPost(e) {
  var sheet = SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSpreadsheet().getActiveSheet();
  if (sheet.getLastRow() === 0) {
    sheet.appendRow(["Timestamp", "Event", "Pupil Name", "Class", "Marks (/10)", "Score", "Accuracy", "Current Q", "Selected", "Correct?", "Q1-Q10 Details"]);
  }
  var data = JSON.parse(e.postData.contents);
  sheet.appendRow([
    new Date(),
    data.eventType || "FINAL_SCORE",
    data.studentName || "Anonymous",
    data.studentClass || "-",
    data.marks + " / " + data.totalQuestions,
    data.score,
    data.accuracy + "%",
    data.currentQuestion || "All Completed",
    data.chosenAnswer || "-",
    data.isCorrect !== undefined ? (data.isCorrect ? "CORRECT" : "WRONG") : "-",
    data.details || ""
  ]);
  return ContentService.createTextOutput(JSON.stringify({status: "success"})).setMimeType(ContentService.MimeType.JSON);
}
